Help us choose — which form feels right?
  • On open — do you want a story first (A), a quick scan of boxes (B), or a list to browse (C)? How fast must you see "Do this next"?
  • Where should waiting Reviews and open Blockers live — in the same text, or in their own box?
  • Is "Why we suggest this" clear enough? Does it help without blocking?
  • In the Skill detail — is Overview vs Evidence vs Materials vs History the right split? Where do LearningResource, Pass eligibility and Review dates belong?
  • Do the soft buttons that are not yet active ("Mark as passed — confirm step comes later") make the next step clear?
  • All three forms show the same idea — daily use only: today, suggestions, skill detail, health, and a clear Passing/Mastering step after you confirm. Other actions (add evidence, add Blocker, set Review) stay in the app you use now and come later.
