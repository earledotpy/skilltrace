# Recognize nonlinear function shapes

## What this skill is

Recognize a quadratic (parabola) and an exponential curve from a graph or table, contrast their rates of change with a line’s constant rate, and describe where each grows fastest.

## Why this skill

Nonlinearity is what activation functions and growth models add over linear ones. Recognizing these shapes — and feeling how their growth differs from a line — prepares every later encounter with curves that accelerate.

## What passing requires

See the artifact spec and validation gate in `evidence/` for this node (`spec.math.functions.nonlinear_functions`); Evidence is judged by learner manual review. The body does not restate them.

## How to work on it

Work graph-reading problems on quadratics and exponential growth, each time contrasting the curve’s changing rate with a line’s steady one. Seed estimate: 30–60 minutes.
