# Work with percentages, ratios, and proportions

## What this skill is

Convert fluently between fractions, decimals, and percentages, compute a percentage of a quantity and a percentage change, and solve a proportion by cross-multiplication.

## Why this skill

Rates, proportions, and percentages are the everyday language of probabilities, growth, and normalized data. Almost every statistics claim you will read is stated in these terms, so reading them fluently is reading the field fluently.

## What passing requires

See the artifact spec and validation gate in `evidence/` for this node (`spec.math.arithmetic.percentages_ratios`); Evidence is judged by learner manual review. The body does not restate them.

## How to work on it

Work conversion and proportion problems with answers, narrating what each percentage is a fraction *of* before computing. Seed estimate: 30–60 minutes.
