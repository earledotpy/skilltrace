# Explain and calculate slope

## What this skill is

Calculate slope from two points and explain it as a rate of change — how much the output moves per unit of input.

## Why this skill

Rate of change recurs in linear functions, trends, and later in gradients: the same rise-over-run idea with larger machinery around it. A slope you can both compute and explain in words transfers to each of those settings.

## What passing requires

See the artifact spec and validation gate in `evidence/` for this node (`spec.math.functions.slope`); Evidence is judged by learner manual review. The body does not restate them.

## How to work on it

Compute rise over run on plotted pairs, then explain each result as a sentence about change before moving to the next pair. Seed estimate: 45–150 minutes.
