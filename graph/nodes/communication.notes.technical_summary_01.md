# Write a concise technical summary

## What this skill is

Summarize a concept, procedure, or blocker in clear technical notes: state the claim, the supporting evidence, the remaining uncertainty, and the next action, in language a future you (or a teammate) can act on.

## Why this skill

Notes that cannot be acted on are not notes; clarity and honesty here compound across every later project.

## What passing requires

See the artifact spec and validation gate in `evidence/` for this node (`spec.communication.notes.technical_summary`); Evidence is judged by learner manual review. The body does not restate them.

## How to work on it

Study short-sentence, point-first writing first, then rewrite one real summary into the claim, evidence, next-step shape. Seed estimate: 20–60 minutes.
