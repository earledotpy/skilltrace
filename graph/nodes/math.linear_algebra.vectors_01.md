# Represent and combine vectors

## What this skill is

Read a vector as an ordered list of numbers and as an arrow from the origin, add two vectors component-by-component, and scale a vector by a constant, explaining what addition and scaling do to the arrow.

## Why this skill

Foundational for every later linear-algebra node: the dot product, matrices, and matrix products are all built on this representation.

## What passing requires

See the artifact spec and validation gate in `evidence/` for this node (`spec.math.linear_algebra.vectors`); Evidence is judged by learner manual review. The body does not restate them.

## How to work on it

Build the geometric picture first, then drill component arithmetic with worked answers until addition and scaling are automatic. Seed estimate: 30–60 minutes.
