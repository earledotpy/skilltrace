# Solve a system of two linear equations

## What this skill is

Solve a system of two linear equations in two unknowns by substitution and by elimination, and interpret the solution as the point where the two lines meet — including the no-solution and infinitely-many cases.

## Why this skill

Several unknowns at once is the shape of real modeling: one equation rarely suffices. Systems are also the algebraic root of the matrix equation Ax = b, so this is where single-equation skill starts becoming linear algebra.

## What passing requires

See the artifact spec and validation gate in `evidence/` for this node (`spec.math.algebra.systems_equations`); Evidence is judged by learner manual review. The body does not restate them.

## How to work on it

Work substitution and elimination problems with answers, sketching the two lines for each system so the algebra stays tied to its picture. Seed estimate: 45–90 minutes.
